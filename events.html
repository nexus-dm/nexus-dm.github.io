Open physics data: what license and attribution terms should it carry?

Public-access rules now require physics collaborations to plan data releases, but none say what license the data should carry, who must be credited, or what a user may build from it. The site documents what collaborations across physics have actually done and highlights the questions that remain open.

Organized by Amy Roberts (CU Denver, SuperCDMS), Michela Taufer (UT Knoxville), and Jessica Walker (UT Knoxville).

Contributions are licensed CC BY 4.0, kept at github.com/det-lab/open-physics-data-terms, and archived at Zenodo with each release.
